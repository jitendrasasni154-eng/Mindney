Comparison Is Making You Poor - The Silent Disease Destroying Your Confidence
"Comparison is a silent disease. You compare your Chapter 1 with someone else's Chapter 20."
1

Comparison Makes You Hollow From Inside

THE REAL DISEASE

Look at the left side of the image. A student sits in a dark room, holding his head, staring at his phone where he sees AIR 1, SDE Google, 50 LPA, Harvard. This is what comparison does. Every time you feel inferior seeing others, your brain releases cortisol. Your confidence drops daily. You look normal outside but become empty inside. You lose interest in your own goals and keep thinking "I am left behind." While you are thinking, others are working and moving ahead. Comparison steals your present and traps you in someone else's life. It stops your growth completely.

2

Everyone Is Born For A Different Purpose

DEEP TRUTH

Every person is born for a different purpose. You cannot copy everyone's journey. Some are born to be painters, some coders, some teachers. But when you compare, you ignore your own design and try to copy others. A fish cannot climb a tree, but in water no one is faster. The work for which you are made, no one can do better than you. That is your superpower. Comparison makes you forget it. You leave your own gold mine and start digging in someone else's mine where you will never find gold.

3

How Society Plants The First Seed Of Comparison

ROOT CAUSE

A child is born confident. Then society plants the first seed with lines like "Look at his marks, look at yours. His son got IIT, what are you doing?" People think it motivates, but it destroys self-trust. The child starts thinking "I am not enough." This thought sits in the subconscious. That seed planted at age 10 becomes a tree of anxiety at age 25. Because of this, the student never runs his own race, he keeps running someone else's race his whole life.

4

Only You Can Defeat You

POWER FORMULA

If you want to compare, compare with yourself. The most powerful truth is - You are the only person who can defeat you. No one else has that power. Can Google SDE defeat you? Can AIR 1 defeat you? No. Only your laziness, comparison and overthinking can defeat you. So ask yourself daily - "Was I better than yesterday?" When you do that, you become unbeatable. The guy on the right side in the image is doing that. His board says GROWTH, FOCUS, CONSISTENCY, PATIENCE, DISCIPLINE. He is tracking his own growth, not others' success.

5

How To Break The Trap? 3 Steps

SOLUTION

Step 1: Mute Noise For 30 Days - Mute accounts that make you feel inferior. Instagram, LinkedIn - wherever comparison triggers. This is self-protection, not hatred. Detox your mind for 30 days.

Step 2: Find Your Own Game - Ask - "Which work makes me lose track of time? Which work can no one do better than me?" Once you find your race, you will stop watching others.

Step 3: Daily 1% Self-Comparison - Every night write - "How was I 1% better than yesterday?" When you win against yourself daily, you will have no time to look at others. Stop comparing, start creating.
