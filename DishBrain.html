How Lab-Grown Brain Cells Learned to Play Pong in 5 Minutes? DishBrain Truth

"We didn't program it to play. It wanted to play. Brain hates unpredictability."
What Actually Happened?
In 2022, an Australian company Cortical Labs made DishBrain. They took 800,000 human brain cells and put them on a chip with electrodes.

This chip was connected to the game Pong. All converted into electrical signals and sent to the brain cells.
Simple Logic - How It Played Pong?
1. Input: Where is the ball? Brain gets electric pulse from that side.

2. Output: Cells fire back and move the paddle up/down.

3. The Trick: Brain hates surprise. Miss = random noisy feedback (punishment). Hit = clean predictable feedback (reward).

So brain learns: "If I hit the ball, world becomes predictable."
