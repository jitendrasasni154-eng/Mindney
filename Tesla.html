How It Works? Compressed Air + Newton's 3rd Law

SCIENCE

There is a COPV tank - same used in SpaceX Falcon rocket. It stores ultra-high pressure air at 10,000 psi. When you press accelerator, valve opens, air comes out supersonic from thrusters. Newton's 3rd Law: Every action has equal opposite reaction. Air goes backward, car goes forward with insane force. 4 thrusters back for forward, 2 under car for lift, 2 front for braking, 2 sides for cornering. No fuel burn, only cold air - safe.
