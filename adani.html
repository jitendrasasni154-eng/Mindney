1

College Dropout to Diamond Trader - 1978

HUMBLE START

Gautam Adani was born in Ahmedabad, middle-class family. Dropped out of college in 2nd year. Went to Mumbai with Rs 10000. Worked as diamond sorter at Zaveri Bazaar for Rs 300/month. In 2 years he learned trading, made his first Rs 1 Lakh. At 18, he went back and started a PVC film factory with his brother with Rs 5 Lakh loan. That's where his real business brain started.

2

Mundra Port - The Masterstroke That Made Him

GAME CHANGER

In 1991, economic reforms came. In 1995, Gujarat govt asked for private port. Everyone said no one will invest in barren Mundra. Adani said yes. He took 3300 acres of wasteland and built India's largest private port. Today Mundra handles 155 Million Tons cargo. This one port gives him Rs 500 Crore per month profit. This is called vision - where others see desert, he saw dollars.

3

From Coal to Airports - Infra King

INFRA EMPIRE

Adani understood one thing - Govt can't do everything. He took sectors where govt needed private help. Coal mining in Australia (Carmichael), 13,000 MW power plants, 6 airports (Mumbai, Lucknow, Ahmedabad), 30% of India's grain stored in Adani silos, 7000 km transmission lines. His logic: Control the backbone, money will follow. Today if you fly, eat, use electricity - you pay Adani somewhere.

4

Real Truth - Debt or Smart Leverage?

REAL TRUTH

People say Adani has huge debt - $30 Billion. But real truth is different. His total assets are $65 Billion. Debt-to-asset is 45% - normal for infra business. Example: You take 80 Lakh loan for 1 Crore flat - that's 80% debt, but you own flat. Same logic. Plus his companies generate Rs 80000 Crore cash every year. After Hindenburg attack in 2023, he lost $50 Billion in 10 days, but recovered it in 11 months by prepaying Rs 20000 Crore debt. That's real comeback.
