kese logo na sarkar ko majboor Kiya

Bagru, Jaipur ka ek school jo 40 saal se kharab tha — logo ne 10 din me sarkar se kaise banwa liya?

Scene kya tha?
Bagru me ek sarkari school 1985 me bana tha. Dheere dheere deewarein crack ho gayi, chhat se paani tapakne laga. 2024 me sarkar ne usko unsafe declare karke band kar diya. Par naya school banaya nahi.

To bacche kaha gaye? Gaon walon ne apne se ek jagah di — bhains ke tabale ke bilkul paas. Waha na chhat, na toilet, na paani. 40 bacche zameen par bori bichha ke padh rahe the. Garmi me loo, barish me keechad.

🔍 Aur ye sirf ek school nahi tha —

Rajasthan me 611 sarkari schools aise hain jinki apni building hi nahi hai. Jaipur me 38, Jhalawar me 23, Barmer me 34.
2047 schools me toilet nahi hai, 1049 me peene ka paani nahi hai. Ye sab sarkar ki apni audit report me nikla hai.

Fir logo ne kya kiya?
Gaon ke ek ladke ne apne phone se video banayi. 1 minute ki video — bacche zameen par, piche bhains. Usne likha #SchoolThikKaro aur CJP (Citizens for Justice and Peace) ko tag kar diya.

CJP ne us video ko uthaya, Dhruv Rathee ne share kiya, aur 3 din me wo video 10 lakh se zyada logo tak pahuch gayi. News channels aaye, ETV Bharat, Free Press Journal ne story cover ki.

28 August: Video viral hui

29 August: Media Bagru pahucha

30 August: DM ne file dekhi, MLA ne notice liya

1 September: ₹18 Lakh ka fund pass

2 September: JCB aayi, purani building todi

"Humne sirf video banayi thi, socha koi dekhega nahi. Par jab pura desh dekhne laga to sarkar ko aana pada." — Gaon ka ladka
PROOF 2: Bagru School ke liye ₹18 Lakh Pass — DMFT se ₹12 Lakh + MLA Fund se ₹6 Lakh
ETV Bharat →

Sarkar majboor kaise hui?
Jab video viral hui to sabse pehle Education Department ko jawab dena pada. Fir DMFT se ₹12 lakh aur MLA fund se ₹6 lakh — total ₹18 lakh Bagru school ki nayi building ke liye pass hua.

Par kahani yahi khatam nahi hui. Jab 611 schools ka data samne aaya to sarkar par pressure aur bada. Fir 2 September ko sarkar ne bada elaan kiya —

💥 Bada Elaan:

₹550 Crore purane schools ko repair karne ke liye + ₹450 Crore naye schools banane ke liye = ₹1000 Crore.

Aur har saal ₹2500 Crore, 5 saal me ₹12,500 Crore schools ke liye.

Ab kya ho raha hai Bagru me?
Purani unsafe building ko JCB se tod diya gaya hai. Wahi zameen par nayi building ka kaam start ho gaya hai. Bacche abhi bhi thode din tabale wali jagah par padhenge, par 3-4 mahine me naye classroom me shift ho jayenge.

Is story se kya seekhne ko milta hai?
Ek phone, ek video, aur ek hashtag ne 40 baccho ki zindagi badal di. Aur 611 schools ka sach desh ke samne la diya. Isiliye kehte hain — jab log ek saath bolte hain to sarkar ko sunna padta hai.
