🛢️ Step 1: Kacha Tel (Crude Oil) Kya Hota Hai?
🛢️ Step 1: What is Crude Oil?
Socho jaise jameen ke andar se kaala gaadha paani nikalta hai, use Crude Oil (Kacha Tel) kehte hain. Is kache tel ko factory me process karke Petrol, Diesel aur Kerosene banaya jata hai.

Duniya bhar ke desho me iska daam har din badalta rehta hai. Iska rate Per Barrel (approx 159 Liters) me count hota hai.
Imagine thick black oil extracted from underground—that is Crude Oil. Refineries process it into Petrol, Diesel, and Jet Fuel.

Global prices fluctuate daily and are traded in units of Per Barrel (approx 159 Liters).
👉 Simple me: Kacha Tel factory me jata hai aur Petrol ban kar nikalta hai.
👉 Simply put: Raw Crude enters refinery and exits as usable Petrol.

📉 Step 2: 2014 Me Kya Hua Aur Aaj Kya Hai?
📉 Step 2: What Happened Since 2014?
Year 2014 me international market me kacha tel bohot mehanga tha—$115 per barrel! Phir bhi India me petrol lagbhag ₹70–₹75/liter bikta tha.

Baad me kacha tel bohot sasta ho gaya ($40–$60 per barrel). Rules ke hisab se petrol ₹40–₹50 hona chahiye tha, lekin daam ₹100 ke paar pahunch gaye!
In 2014, global crude reached a high of $115 per barrel, yet petrol in India was ₹70–₹75/liter.

Later, crude crashed to $40–$60 per barrel. Logically, fuel prices should have dropped to ₹40–₹50, but retail petrol crossed ₹100 instead!
👉 Simple me: Kacha tel sasta hua, par Petrol ka daam double ho gaya.
👉 Simply put: Global oil got cheaper, but petrol became twice as costly.

🏛️ Step 3: Tax Ka Khel - Petrol Sasta Kyun Nahi Hua?
🏛️ Step 3: The Tax Mechanism - Why Didn't Prices Drop?
Jab crude oil sasta hua, toh Government ne petrol par Excise Duty (TAX) bohot zyada badha diya.

Aap jo ₹100 ka petrol khareedte ho, usme asli petrol ka cost sirf ₹50–₹55 hota hai. Baaki ka ₹45–₹50 TAX hota hai!
When crude prices plunged, government duties and Taxes were heavily increased.

Out of every ₹100 spent at pump, baseline fuel cost is roughly ₹50–₹55, while remaining is Government Tax!
👉 Simple me: Aadha paisa fuel ka, Aadha paisa Government Tax!
👉 Simply put: Half for fuel, Half directly to taxes!

🌽 Step 4: Ethanol E20 Kya Hai Aur Vaada Kya Tha?
🌽 Step 4: What is E20 Ethanol Blending?
Ethanol ek biofuel hai jo Sugarcane (Ganna) aur Corn (Makka) se banta hai. Plan tha ki 80% Petrol + 20% Ethanol milakar E20 Fuel banayein.

Vaada: Ethanol cheap hai, toh petrol sasta milega.

Asliyat: E20 petrol poore desh me bik raha hai, par daam 1 rupaya bhi kam nahi hua!
Ethanol is a biofuel distilled from Sugarcane and Maize. Under E20 policy, petrol is blended with 20% Ethanol.

The Promise: Lower production cost & cheaper retail fuel.

The Reality: E20 is widely used, yet retail prices didn't see any price reduction!
👉 Simple me: Blending se cost kam hui, par public ko discount nahi mila.
👉 Simply put: Production costs dropped, but consumers received no discount.

🍬 Step 5: Side Effect - Sugar (Cheeni) Mehangi Kyun Hui?
🍬 Step 5: Side Effect - Why Did Sugar Prices Spike?
Ethanol ke liye bohot saara ganna aur Sugar Syrup divert kar diya gaya. Isse khane wali cheeni ke liye ganna kam pad gaya!

Supply kam hone se Sugar prices rapidly rise ho gaye. Government ko export ban karna pada tab jaakar rates control hue.
Diverting sugarcane for ethanol limited supply for sugar refining.

This bottleneck caused domestic sugar inflation before export bans stabilized rates.
👉 Simple me: Fuel ke chakkar me Sugar bhi mehangi ho gayi!
👉 Simply put: Biofuel push accidentally triggered sugar inflation!
