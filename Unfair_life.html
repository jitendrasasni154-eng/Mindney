Why Crime Is Increasing In India? Real Reason & Simple Solution
"Look, we all see daily news about theft and fights. So let's talk politely - why is this happening and how can we stay safe? No hate, only awareness."
1

See, What's Actually Happening Around Us?

POLITE INTRO

Friends, if you open news daily, you will see some crime news for sure. So many people ask - is our country safe or not? See, my point is simple, this article is just to make you aware and educated. I am not blaming any government or any person here. Just let's understand the situation politely.

2

First Reason - System Thoda Loose Hai

SYSTEM ISSUE

Look, sometimes what happens is, police or system takes too much time to take action. And when action is slow, some wrong people think - "chalo koi dar nahi hai". So they get more confident. If law is strict and fast, people will automatically have fear to do wrong. That's the main thing. We need quick and strong action.

3

Second Reason - Ghar Ka Mahaul Matters A Lot

FAMILY MATTERS

Second thing which I personally feel is - how a child is grown at home. See, if at home there is no proper guidance, no good values, and the child is always with wrong friends, then obviously he can go to wrong path. It's very simple - Good upbringing = Good thinking. So parents' role is very very important here.

Solution - How Can We Stay Safe?

SIMPLE SOLUTION

So what can we do? 3 simple things. 1. Be Aware: Learn some basic self-defence, it's very useful. 2. Avoid Unnecessary Fights: See, smart person always avoids fight with unknown people. No need to argue on road. 3. Support Strict Law: We should all want that law should be strict so wrong people have fear. Simple formula - Be Aware + Stay Away From Fights + Respect Law.

5

Final Message - Be Alert, Not Scared

FINAL NOTE

See friends, crime is there in every country, but we can reduce it. Don't be scared, just be alert. Don't go alone to unknown places at night, keep emergency numbers, and if you see something wrong, call 112. This article is only for education, to make you aware. So stay safe and keep your family safe.
