Why 90% Businesses Fail in First Year?

5 mistakes every beginner makes. Avoid them.

1 No Backup

Business eats money for first 6 months. No backup = shop closed.

Fix: Monthly Expense x 6 = Always keep separate.

2 Selling to Everyone

If you sell to everyone, you sell to no one. Pick one customer.

Fix: 1 Age, 1 City, 1 Problem - Focus only there.

3 Free / Udhaar to Friends

Discount and freebies closed 90% shops. Business is not charity.

Fix: Rule - No Free, No Udhaar.

4 Zero Marketing

Best product with zero marketing = zero sales.

Fix: Daily 1 Reel + 2 WhatsApp Status.

5 Quit Too Fast

No result in 2 months so quit. Business needs 1 year.

Fix: Promise - I will not quit for 1 year.

Pehle Saal Me 90% Business Fail Kyu Hote Hai?

5 galtiyan jo har beginner karta hai. Tu mat karna.

1 Backup Nahi Hai

Business pehle 6 mahine sirf khata hai. Backup nahi toh band.

Fix: Mahine ka kharcha x 6 = Alag rakho.

2 Sabko Bechna Hai

Jo sabko bechta hai wo kisi ko nahi bechta. Ek hi customer pakdo.

Fix: 1 Umar, 1 Sheher, 1 Problem - bas wahi.

3 Dosti Me Free / Udhaar

Discount ne 90% dukaan band karayi hai.

Fix: Rule - No Free, No Udhaar.

4 Marketing Zero

Product acha hai par bataya hi nahi toh sale kaha se ayegi?

Fix: Roz 1 reel + 2 status.

5 Jaldi Haar Gaye

2 mahine me result nahi toh quit. Business ko 1 saal de.

Fix: 1 saal tak quit nahi karunga - likh ke laga de.
