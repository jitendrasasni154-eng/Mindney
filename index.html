Section 2: Startup Psychology - Think Like a Millionaire

RULE 1

Be an Owner, Not a Consumer

Consumers scroll Instagram and buy things.
Owners scroll Instagram and ask "How can I sell on this?".

Consumers spend money on iPhone EMI.
Owners spend money on skills that buy 10 iPhones.

Question to ask daily: "Am I building an Asset or buying a Liability?"

RULE 2

Asset vs Liability - The Rich Secret

Liability: Takes money OUT of your pocket. Car EMI, Expensive Phone, Partying, Netflix.

Asset: Puts money INTO your pocket. This Website, YouTube Channel, A Skill, A Business.

Rule before age 25: 90% of your money and time should go to buying Assets only.

RULE 3

Fail Fast and Fail Cheap

Your first 3 business ideas WILL fail. And that is perfect.

Fail in 1 week with ₹0, not in 1 year with ₹5 Lakhs. Each failure gives you data. Use that data to build business number 4.

Remember: Every millionaire has 10 failed businesses behind them.

RULE 4

Delayed Gratification

Poor people want results TODAY.
Rich people work for 1 year to get results for 10 years.

Can you post 1 video daily for 90 days without any results? Can you learn 1 skill for 3 months without earning?
If yes, you will beat 99% of people.
