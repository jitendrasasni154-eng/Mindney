CHAPTER 01

Business karna dukaan nahi, problem solve karna hai.

Is ek line me poora business chhupa hai. 90% log isko galat samajhte hai, isiliye fail hote hai.

Business is not opening a shop, it's solving a problem.

This one line has whole business hidden. 90% people get it wrong, that's why they fail.

🥵 Step 1: Pyaas hai (Problem dhoondho)

Socho maidaan me 20 bacche khel rahe hai. Garmi tez hai. Sabko pyaas lagi hai. Paani ka nal kharab hai, ghar door hai. Sab pareshan hai, khelna band ho gaya hai.

Yehi PROBLEM hai. Business ki bhasha me isko Demand kehte hai. Jab tak dard nahi hai, business nahi hai. Pehle dard ko dekho, usko feel karo. Tumhare aas paas bhi aise 100 dard hai.

👉 Simple me: Bina pyaas ke paani ka koi matlab nahi.

🥵 Step 1: Thirst is there (Find Problem)

20 kids in ground, very hot, everyone thirsty. Tap broken, home far. Game stopped. This is PROBLEM. In business this is called Demand. No pain, no business.

💧 Step 2: Paani laaye (Apni skill se solution do)

Ab ek baccha dimag lagata hai. Wo sochta hai "mai daud sakta hu". Wo bhaag ke ghar se ek bada jug aur glass le aata hai. Aur sabko thanda paani pilata hai.

Usne kya kiya? Usne apni skill (daudna) se dusro ka dard khatam kiya. Yehi SOLUTION hai. Tumhari skill coding ho sakti hai, video banana ho sakta hai, bolna ho sakta hai. Bas us skill se kisi ka dard khatam karo.

👉 Simple me: Tumhe jo aata hai, usi se kisi ki help karo.

💧 Step 2: Brought Water (Give Solution)

One kid thinks "I can run". He brings big jug and glasses. He solved pain with his skill. This is SOLUTION. Your skill can be coding, video, speaking. Use it to help.

💰 Step 3: Inaam mila (Business ban gaya)

Ab sab bacche khush hai. Khel fir se shuru ho gaya hai. Sab us bacche ko bolte hai "Thank you! Ye le 10 rupaye, tune hamari madad ki".

Us bacche ne paise nahi maange the. Paise uski help ka inaam hai. Yahi business hai.

Galat tarika: Pehle bottle kharido, sticker lagao, fir bolo "kharido". Koi nahi kharidega.
Sahi tarika: Pehle pyaas dhoondho, fir paani do. Paise apne aap aa jayenge.

👉 Simple me: Business paise ke liye nahi, problem solve karne ke liye hota hai. Paise toh bonus hai.

💰 Step 3: Got Reward (Business is done)

Now kids happy, game started again. They say "Thank you! Take 10 Rs". He didn't ask for money. Money is reward for help. That's business.

🔥 Ab tumhari baari - Real Life Example

Dard: Mawana me Sharma ji ki kirana dukaan hai. Customer aate hai par online order nahi le paate.
Tumhari Skill: Tumhe website banana aata hai.
Solution: Tum ek simple page bana dete ho jisme "WhatsApp pe Order Karo" button hai.
Business: Sharma ji khush, roz 5 order extra. Tumhe 2000 rupaye dete hai.

Zomato ne bhookh ka dard, Ola ne auto ka dard khatam kiya. Tumhe bhi aas paas ka ek dard dhoondhna hai.

🔥 Your Turn - Real Example

Pain: Grocery shop can't take online orders.
Your Skill: You know website.
Business: You make WhatsApp order site, charge 2000. Simple.
