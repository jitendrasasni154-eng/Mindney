Bhai, Cheeni Itni Mehngi Kyun Ho Gayi?

1. Teri Cheeni Ab Bike Me Jal Rahi Hai

Dekh bhai, isko aise samajh. Ganna se 2 cheez banti hai - ek cheeni, dusri sharab jaisa petrol jisko ethanol bolte hain.

Sarkar ne bola ki petrol me ye ethanol milao taaki petrol sasta ho. Sunne me achha laga. Par hua kya? Mill walo ne socha ethanol me zyada paisa hai, to unhone 30 lakh ton cheeni ko cheeni banaya hi nahi, sidha petrol bana diya.

Matlab samajh, tere ghar me 10 toffee thi, tu 3 toffee khud kha gaya aur 7 toffee cycle me daal di. Ab ghar me toffee kam ho gayi na? Bas wahi cheeni ke saath hua hai. Jo cheeni teri chai me aani thi, wo ab teri bike me jal rahi hai. Isiliye dukan me cheeni kam hai aur daam zyada hai.

👉 Simple me: Cheeni kam nahi hui, cheeni ko petrol bana diya. Mast baat hai na?

2. Mill Wale Maal Rok Ke Baithe Hain - Sabse Bada Game

Ab ye sabse important point hai, dhyan se sun mere bhai. Mill matlab bade factory wale, jaise Balrampur Chini, Dalmia wale. Ye log UP aur Maharashtra ke bade log hain.

Sarkar inko har mahine bolti hai - tum is mahine itni hi cheeni bech sakte ho, taaki daam control me rahe. Isko quota bolte hain. Par in logo ne kya kiya? Sarkar ne bola 201 lakh ton becho, inhone 214 lakh ton bech diya. Matlab pehle zyada bech ke paisa kama liya.

Ab inke godam me bahut kam cheeni bachi hai. To ye kya kar rahe hain? Ye cheeni ko rok ke baithe hain. Soch rahe hain, abhi nahi bechenge, Ganesh Chaturthi aur Diwali pe jab mithai banegi tab 62 Rs/kg pe bechenge. Isiliye 10 din me hi daam 47 Rs se 62 Rs ho gaya.

Sarkar khud bol rahi hai ye daam badhna galat hai, natural nahi hai. Ye jaan bujh ke roka hua hai. Full to full game hai bhai!

👉 Simple me: Dukan wale ne toffee chhupa di, taaki baad me mehngi bech sake. Chalak hai bhai ye log!

3. Bahar Bech Di, Ab Yahan Mehngi

Ek aur bewakoofi dekho. Jab desh me hi cheeni kam thi, ganna kharab ho gaya tha Red Rot bemari se, tab bhi in mill walo ne 8 lakh ton cheeni bahar desh me bech di. Bahar bechne se inko zyada dollar milte hain na.

Ab kya hua? Desh me stock ekdum khali ho gaya. 30 saal me sabse kam stock bacha hai, sirf 35 lakh ton. Jabki hame chahiye 50 lakh ton.

To ab samjha? Pehle bahar bech di, ab yahan bol rahe hain cheeni kam hai. Isiliye sarkar ko ab bahar se cheeni mangwani pad rahi hai.

4. Tere Liye Iska Matlab Kya Hai?

Dekh bhai, tu soch raha hoga isse mujhe kya? Bhai bahut matlab hai.

Matlab ye sab kya, kam mehnga tha jo upar se cheeni bhi mehngi kar di? Pehle hi sab mehnga hai, ab chai bhi mehngi ho gayi. Ek gareeb aadmi jo din me 3 chai peeta hai, uska mahine ka 200 Rs extra ja raha hai.

Par tu tension mat le bhai. Tu to smart hai. Baki log bas mehngi cheeni kharid rahe hain, tu uska reason samajh gaya. Ye high daam bas November tak hai. Jaise hi naya ganna aayega, daam wapas 45-48 pe aa jayega. Tab tak thoda kam chai pee le, ya dost ke ghar ja ke pee le, mast plan hai!

Dekh bhai, sab mehnga tha hi, upar se cheeni bhi mehngi kar di. Par tu alag hai. Tu wo banda hai jo reason samajhta hai, bheed me nahi chalta.
