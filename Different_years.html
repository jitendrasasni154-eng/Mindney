Why The Same Day Is Different Year In Different Countries?

"For us it's 2026, but for someone in Thailand it's 2569 and for someone in Ethiopia it's still 2019 - same day, different years."
1 Ethiopia - Still in 2019 2019 EC

They follow Coptic Calendar, 7-8 years behind. They have 13 months. They never accepted Pope's correction in 1582.
2 Thailand - Year 2569 2569 BE

Counts from Lord Buddha's Death. Gregorian + 543 = Buddhist Era. So 2026+543=2569.
3 Japan - Reiwa 8 Reiwa 8

New Emperor = New calendar. Reiwa started in 2019 with Emperor Naruhito.
4 Saudi / Islamic - 1448 AH 1448 Hijri

Starts from 622 AD migration of Prophet Muhammad. Moon calendar - 354 days, 11 days shorter.
5 Israel - 5787 AM 5787

Hebrew calendar counts from creation of world.
6 Iran - 1405 SH 1405

Solar Hijri - Starts from Prophet migration but counts Sun years. New Year Nowruz on 21 March.
7 North Korea - Juche 115 115

Counts from birth of Kim Il-sung 1912. So 2026-1911=115.
