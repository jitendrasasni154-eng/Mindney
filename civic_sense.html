Lack Of Civic Sense Causes Danger - Why We Are The Dirtiest On Roads
"Civic sense is not about cleaning. It is about thinking - Will my one action make someone else's life difficult?"
1

How Much Lack? Almost Zero In Public

REALITY

We keep our house clean, but throw garbage outside our gate. We think road is government's, not ours. When everyone thinks like this, one wrapper becomes a mountain of garbage. That garbage blocks drains and causes floods. When everyone does wrong, wrong feels normal. Even educated people do same. It is about mindset, not education.

2

Where Is Lack? Everywhere - Road, Garbage, Queue, Noise

WHERE WE FAIL

Wrong side driving to save 100m, jumping red light, parking on footpath, unnecessary honking. Throwing packets from car, spitting paan on walls, breaking queues, writing names on monuments, stealing taps, loudspeakers till 2 AM. We confused freedom with no responsibility. Real freedom is doing anything without harming others.

3

Why Only India? 5 Deep Causes

ROOT CAUSES

1. Chalta Hai Culture: We normalized wrong.
2. No Ownership: My house is mine, road is govt's.
3. No Punishment: Singapore fine $1000, here no fine.
4. No Education: Schools never teach civic sense.
5. Parenting: Child sees father throwing bottle, learns it is normal. We actively teach our children to lack civic sense.

4

Whose Fault? Government Or People? Both

TRUTH

Government fault - not enough dustbins, no heavy fines, drains not cleaned, no subject in schools. 50% fault government's.

But 50% ours. Even where dustbin is there, we throw outside. Even where footpath is there, we park bike. If we throw, no government can keep India clean. If we stop throwing, India will be clean in 30 days.

5

Why Japan, Singapore, Germany Are Clean?

LEARNING

In Japan, children clean classrooms from age 5 - your mess is your responsibility. In Singapore, littering fine $1000 + community cleaning in I Littered jacket. In Germany, people follow rules even at 2 AM when no one is watching. Developed countries are rich because they are disciplined. Discipline came first, money came later.

6

How To Fix? How Next Gen Can Learn?

SOLUTION - 3 LEVELS

Level 1 - Individual: Keep polybag in car/bike. Don't throw anything for 30 days, even wrapper. Becomes habit. Don't honk unnecessarily.

Level 2 - Family & School: Don't throw in front of child. Make child use dustbin from age 3. Schools should make civic sense daily practice like Japan - clean classroom once a week. Children learn by doing.

Level 3 - Govt: Heavy fines like Singapore - spitting Rs 500, littering Rs 1000. Dustbins every 100m, proper footpaths, civic sense as subject from Class 1.

Country becomes clean not when govt puts more sweepers, but when citizens throw less garbage.
