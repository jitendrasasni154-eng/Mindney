4 Neuroscience Facts Every Kid Must Know

"Your brain is not fixed. You build it daily."
1 Morning Brain Takes Best Decisions

Your Prefrontal Cortex is fresh in morning. After 100s of small decisions its energy drops, this is Decision Fatigue. That's why judges give better decisions in morning.

Tip: Do hard study in morning, sleep early. Don't waste best brain power on phone.
2 You Become Who You Stay With

Neuroscience calls it Neural Coupling. Your Mirror Neurons copy brain patterns of people you stay with. Two brains sync like WiFi.

Tip: You are average of 5 people you stay with. Choose circle wisely.
3 You Build Your Brain Daily

This is Neural Plasticity. Daily habits make new wires. Do coding daily, brain makes strong coding circuit. Same for overthinking.

Tip: Talent is not fixed, it's daily practice.
4 Your Brain Hears Your Voice As Someone Else's

When you say "I can't do this, it's too big for me", brain hears it as if another person is telling you. That's why negative self-talk destroys confidence.

Tip: Always say "I can do it, I will figure it out". Self-talk is brain programming.

4. Talk to yourself like your best friend

Tip: Mushkil kaam subah karo, jaldi so jao.

Ise Neural Plasticity kehte hain. Roz coding karoge toh uska strong circuit ban jayega.

Tip: Koi talented paida nahi hota, practice se banta hai.

Ye self-talk nahi, programming hai.
