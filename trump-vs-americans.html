Section 1: What is ICE and What is Its Work?
RULE 1

ICE - The Agency That Was Doing Its Job

ICE stands for Immigration and Customs Enforcement. It was created in 2003 after the 9/11 attacks under the Department of Homeland Security (DHS). Its official work was to handle immigration enforcement, deport people with criminal backgrounds, stop human trafficking, and secure US borders.

Initially, ICE was doing its work normally. It was seen as a normal law enforcement agency. No big controversies, just border security and deportation of criminals.

Section 2: How The Game Changed
RULE 2

The 3000 Arrests Order

According to many news reports in 2025, the Trump administration gave a direct order to ICE that every day 3000 arrests must be done. This order created huge pressure on ICE agents.

Because of this pressure, ICE agents started raiding grocery stores, factories, farms, streets and catching anyone they saw. They started arresting innocent people also just to show numbers on paper. It became a numbers game.

RULE 3

Unlimited Power and Fear

As per reports, Trump gave ICE agents power that they can arrest anyone on the basis of suspicion and no legal action can be taken against them because it is a direct government order.

One viral case that is being shared as proof: A 32-year-old lady was shot dead by agents. She was just returning home after dropping her child to school. Agents told her to stop her car, she didn't hear it, and they directly shot her.
