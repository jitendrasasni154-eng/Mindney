The Real Meaning of Study and Education is Not Marks

"Education is not about getting a degree, it's about making yourself capable of living life."

🎯
Percentage is The Biggest Scam

After 10 years, no one will ask what was your 10th percentage. Your marksheet only tells how you wrote for 3 hours on one day. It never tells how wise you are in life, how you handle tough times, how you talk to people.


That's why percentage is a trap. We get stuck in this loop and forget the real meaning of study.

💡
Study vs Education

📚 Study = WorkOpening books, memorizing, giving exams. Taking information.
🧠 Education = MindsetUnderstanding right and wrong, learning to think, understanding life. Transformation.

What Real Education Teaches?

Study tells you that the world is round. Education tells you how to live in that round world.

1. To Think - Don't follow the crowd blindly
2. To Question - Ask 'Why?' on everything
3. To Connect - Connect study with life
4. To Solve - Don't cry, find a solution
"Study can give you a job,
but Education teaches you how to live life."

Padhai Ka Asli Matlab Marks Nahi, Zindagi Hai

"Padhai ka matlab apne aap ko is zindagi ko banane ke kabil banana hai."

🎯
Percentage Sabse Bada Scam Hai

10 saal baad koi nahi puchega ki 10th me kitne percent aaye the. Marksheet sirf ye batati hai ki tune ek din 3 ghante me kaisa likha. Wo ye kabhi nahi batati ki tu zindagi me kitna samajhdar hai, tu mushkil time pe kaise ladta hai.


Isiliye percentage ek ganda loop hai. Jisme hum fas jaate hain aur padhai ka asli matlab bhool jaate hain.

💡
Study vs Education

📚 Study = KaamKitab kholna, ratta maarna, exam dena. Information lena.
🧠 Education = SochSahi-galat samajhna, sochna seekhna, zindagi samajhna.

Asli Education Kya Sikhati Hai?

Study batati hai duniya gol hai. Education samjhati hai us gol duniya me kaise jeena hai.

1. Sochna - Bheed ka hissa mat bano
2. Sawal Karna - Har baat par 'Kyun?'
3. Connect Karna - Life se jodna
4. Solution Nikalna - Rona nahi
"Study tumhe naukri dila sakti hai,
par Education tumhe zindagi jeena sikhati hai."
