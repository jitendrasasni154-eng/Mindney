Why Did UN Change The World Map After 500 Years? Equal Earth Truth

"For 500 years we studied a wrong map. Greenland looked as big as Africa, but in reality Africa is 14 times bigger. Now UN fixed it."
1 What Happened?

In August, African Union demanded UN to stop using Mercator Map (1569). They said this map makes Africa look small and Europe big. It's colonial mindset.

UN accepted and launched "Correct The Map" campaign. Now UN recommends schools and offices to use Equal Earth Projection which shows true size of all countries.
2 Old Map - Mercator (1569) - Why It Was Wrong?

Gerardus Mercator made it 500 years ago for sailors, not for students. For ships, it kept directions straight. But to do that, it stretched north and south poles.

❌ MERCATOR

Greenland = Africa
Europe = Big
Africa = Small
Good for: Sailing
✅ EQUAL EARTH

Africa = 14x Greenland
Real size
Real shape
Good for: Learning
Result: Kids thought Africa is small, so Africa is not important. That's psychological bias.
3 New Map - Equal Earth (2018) - Why It's Right?

Made in 2018 by Tom Patterson, Bojan Savric. NASA and many NGOs already use it.

- It shows true relative size - Africa is huge, Russia is not that huge.
- It looks beautiful like round Earth, not stretched.
- It has no political bias - North is not shown as more important.

UN says: Equal Earth = True Size, True World.

4 What Will Change For Us?

1. School books will change in next 2-3 years.
2. Google Maps still uses Mercator for navigation, but classroom wall maps will change to Equal Earth.
3. Kids will learn correct geography, correct importance of Global South - India, Africa, South America.

500 Saal Baad UN Ne Duniya Ka Naksha Kyu Badal Diya?

"500 saal se hum galat naksha padh rahe the. Greenland Africa jitna bada dikhta tha, par asli me Africa usse 14 guna bada hai."
1 Hua Kya Hai?

African Union ne bola - Mercator Map (1569) Africa ko chota dikhata hai, Europe ko bada. Ye colonial soch hai.

UN ne maan liya aur Correct The Map campaign launch kiya. Ab schools ko Equal Earth Map use karne ko bola hai.
2 Purana Naksha - Mercator - Galat Kyu Tha?

Ye 500 saal pehle jahazo ke liye bana tha, baccho ke liye nahi. Direction sahi rakhne ke liye usne upar-neeche ke desho ko kheech ke bada kar diya.

Isliye Greenland jo chota hai, Africa jitna bada dikhne laga. Baccho ko laga Africa chota hai toh important nahi hai. Ye soch galat hai.
3 Naya Naksha - Equal Earth - Sahi Kyu Hai?

2018 me bana. NASA use karta hai.
- Asli size dikhata hai - Africa bahut bada hai.
- Gol aur sundar dikhta hai.
- Kisi desh ko bada-chota dikhane ka bias nahi.

UN ka tagline: True Size, True World.
4 Humare Liye Kya Badlega?

School ki kitabe 2-3 saal me badlegi. Navigation me Google abhi bhi purana use karega, par deewar wala naksha naya hoga. Bacche sahi geography seekhenge - India, Africa ki sahi importance samjhenge.
