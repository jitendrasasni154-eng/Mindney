POOR TRAP

Rich Invests Till Last Breath - Poor Works Till Last Breath

LEGENDARY MINDSET

You said - "Jab tak saanse chal rahi hai, tab tak invest karta rahega". Poor works 30 years, retires with nothing. Rich invests 30 years, his money works for him. Warren Buffett is 94 and still investing. Poor stops earning when he stops working. Rich never stops because his money never sleeps.

3

Business Is Love, Not Occupation

REAL BUSINESS

Business ek pyaar hai jo aap apne kaam se karte ho. Poor sees business as “Kaam karna padega”. Rich sees it as “Kaam karna hai”. Poor wants Friday evening. Rich wants Monday morning. When you love your work, you don't count hours. And when you don't count hours, money counts itself.

RICH SOURCE
