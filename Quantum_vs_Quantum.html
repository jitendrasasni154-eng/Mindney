Quantum Physics vs Quantum Mechanics - Same or Different?

"Mechanics is the rulebook, Physics is the whole game played with that rulebook."

One Line Difference

Quantum Mechanics is a subject / mathematical tool. It tells HOW particles behave. Formulas like Schrödinger Equation.

Quantum Physics is the full big field. It includes Mechanics + its applications like Quantum Computing, Entanglement, Black Holes.

Example: Mechanics = Grammar Rules. Physics = Full English Literature.
Depth - 3 Layers Explained

1. Quantum Mechanics (The Core - 1900)
Deals with electrons, photons. Rules: Uncertainty Principle, Superposition. Pure math.

2. Quantum Physics (The Application)
When we use mechanics to explain: Why sun shines? Why your mobile chip works? What is tunneling? That's Physics.

3. Quantum Field Theory (Advanced)
Mechanics fails at light speed. So QFT says particles are vibrations in fields. Used in CERN.

✅ When To Use Which Word?

1. Talking about formulas & equations → Say Quantum Mechanics.
2. Talking about tech, universe, reality → Say Quantum Physics.
3. You study Mechanics first, then you become Quantum Physicist.
4. Mechanics = Engine. Physics = Whole Car + Road + Driving.
"Mechanics tells you particles CAN be in two places. Physics tells you WHY it changes reality."
